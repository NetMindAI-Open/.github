🔗 **Welcome AI Innovators!**

NetMindAI-Open, proudly driven by [NetMind.AI](https://netmind.ai), is your open-source hub for the next generation of AI infrastructure and agent innovation. We unite developers, researchers, students, and creators to collectively shape the future of practical, scalable, and interoperable AI.

Our open-source collaboration focuses on three strategic pillars:

1. **🚀 AI Service-based MCP Servers:**  
   We develop meaningful AI services packaged into community-friendly MCP servers, enabling various large language models to easily integrate and utilize these services. Our goal is to actively support and adopt standardized protocols, fostering broader model interoperability.

2. **🤖 Curated Agent2Agent Protocol-Compliant Agents:**  
   A curated collection of agents compliant with standardized Agent2Agent communication protocols, facilitating seamless interaction and collaboration across various AI agent frameworks.

3. **🎓 Projects Supporting Academia & Open-Source Community:**  
   Resources, tools, and project frameworks tailored to empower academic researchers and open-source contributors, nurturing the next wave of AI talent and innovation.

We don’t just envision an AI-powered future—we build it openly, collaboratively, and sustainably.
